Demand Analysis

Global consumption, China/India, seasonal patterns & refining

China & India

These two countries drive the majority of incremental global oil demand. Watch import data, refining throughput, and economic indicators (PMI, industrial production).

Seasonal Demand

Driving season (summer) boosts gasoline. Winter increases heating oil/diesel demand. Shoulder seasons can be weaker.

Refining & Products

US refinery utilization, crack spreads (gasoline & diesel), and product inventories give early signals of demand strength or weakness.

How to Trade This Angle

Bullish Demand
  • • Strong China imports
  • • High US gasoline demand
  • • Rising refinery runs
Bearish Demand
  • • Weak Chinese data
  • • Recession fears
  • • Soft product demand
