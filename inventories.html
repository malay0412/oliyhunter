Inventories Analysis

EIA weekly data — the most watched short-term driver

Why Inventories Matter

Weekly EIA Petroleum Status Report (every Wednesday) is the single most important short-term catalyst for crude prices. Large draws = bullish, large builds = bearish. Cushing (delivery point for WTI) is especially important.

Key Numbers to Watch

  • Crude oil stocks (total commercial)
  • Cushing, Oklahoma stocks
  • Gasoline & Distillate stocks
  • Strategic Petroleum Reserve (SPR) changes
  • Implied demand calculations

Trading the Report

Markets often price in consensus estimates before the release. The reaction is to the surprise vs expectations, not the absolute number.

Bullish

Larger-than-expected draw in crude or products

Bearish

Larger-than-expected build
