Geopolitical Risks

Middle East, Russia, sanctions, shipping & supply disruption risk

Key Risk Zones

  • Middle East (Strait of Hormuz, Red Sea, Iran, Israel, Saudi)
  • Russia / Ukraine (sanctions, shadow fleet, export volumes)
  • Libya, Nigeria, Venezuela production volatility
  • Shipping & insurance costs (war risk premiums)

How Risk Premiums Work

Geopolitical events often add a risk premium to oil prices. The size depends on actual or threatened supply disruption. Markets can overreact short-term and then fade the premium if supply remains intact.
