OPEC+, US shale, production, exports & spare capacity

OPEC+ Production

OPEC+ remains the primary swing producer. Watch compliance rates, voluntary cuts, and any unexpected production increases from Saudi Arabia, Russia, UAE, Iraq, or Kazakhstan.

  • Current target vs actual output
  • Compliance percentage
  • Spare capacity (mainly Saudi & UAE)
  • Next JMMC / full meeting dates

US Shale & Production

US crude production is a major source of non-OPEC supply growth. Key metrics: weekly production estimates, Baker Hughes rig count, and Permian Basin activity.

  • EIA weekly US crude production
  • Active oil rigs (Baker Hughes)
  • DUC wells & completion rates
  • Export volumes (US is now a major exporter)

How to Trade This Angle

Bullish Supply Signals
  • • Unexpected OPEC+ cut extension
  • • Production outages (libya, nigeria, etc.)
  • • Falling US rig count
Bearish Supply Signals
  • • OPEC+ raising output targets
  • • Strong US production growth
  • • High compliance failures (overproduction)
