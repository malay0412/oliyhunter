Macro & USD

Dollar strength, interest rates, global growth

USD Index (DXY)

Oil is priced in dollars. A stronger USD usually pressures oil prices lower (and vice versa). Correlation is not perfect but is one of the most consistent macro relationships.

Interest Rates & Growth

Higher rates and tighter financial conditions can slow global growth and reduce oil demand. Fed, ECB, and China policy decisions matter.
